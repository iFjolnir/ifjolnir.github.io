Apps.

Small, single-purpose tools built to solve problems from my own daily life. Each one runs in your browser and saves your progress on your own device.

All apps

  • Vocab Drill Custom vocabulary decks for any purpose, practised across several drill modes with spaced repetition. A multi-library system keeps decks for class, exams and personal interests separate but close at hand, and import/export makes them easy to share.
  • TOPIK 채점 A self-grading companion for TOPIK past papers. Enter your answers, check them against the key, and see your score, without flipping pages and squinting at the answer table.
  • Wongoji Sim Manuscript paper in the browser. Paste a draft, set the line length and a goal range, and the grid handles indent, punctuation and box count, then exports the sheet as a PDF or image with an optional title, name and date.
  • Grammar Drill Build custom grammar patterns practice sets across several drill modes, with misses weighted so weak points come back sooner. Sets stay separate in a library or can be pooled into one run.
  • Essay Check Recall and transcription practice for a saved essay. Write from the outline or copy the master text, then check the draft for missing, extra and near-miss words, scored as a percentage that ignores stray commas.
  • Hanja Recall Sino-Korean morpheme recall. Each card keeps a character, its reading, a gloss. Create sets and practice as cards, a homophone board or a sprint.
  • Okay. A button that hands you one dry line at a time. A smoke break companion for bad days.
  • Fossilis Tracker Set tracking for the board game Fossilis. Tap a category to count fossils toward a set of three, hold to undo, and the score updates as sets complete.
